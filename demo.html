NeoDonkey

Open a company. Post a booking. Read the git log. All in your browser.

1 Create your company

This creates a git repository in your browser's private storage (OPFS). No server. No cloud. No account required.

2 Adopt chart of accounts (SKR04)

Creates the German SKR04 chart with key accounts: 1200 (Bank), 8400 (Revenue), etc.

3 Post your first booking

Classic double-entry: 8400 (Revenue) debit 1200 (Bank) credit €1,000.00. Every posting is validated against the operating model.

4 Audit trail — the git log

Every document is a signed commit. Tamper-evident by design.

What just happened

Git-Native

Every booking is a commit. Full history, branching, and audit trail built-in.

Signed Documents

Ed25519 signatures on every commit. Cryptographic proof of who did what when.

Zero Dependencies

No build step. No Docker. No npm install. Just open the HTML file.

Exact Money

"1000.00 EUR" stored as BigInt minor units. No float anywhere in the ledger.

Operating Model

Business rules live in markdown. The parser enforces them at runtime.

Browser-First

Runs entirely in your browser using OPFS. Your data never leaves your machine.
