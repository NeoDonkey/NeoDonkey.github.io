Why "Free" Is Sustainable

No Servers to Pay For

Traditional ERP vendors charge you for their cloud infrastructure. NeoDonkey runs on your hardware — your laptop, your office PC, your tablet. We have no servers. We have no cloud bill. Therefore, we have nothing to charge you for.

No Support to Sell

NeoDonkey is designed to be understood. The entire system is a git repo you can read. The documentation is the code. When something is simple enough to understand, you don't need a €200/hour consultant to explain it.

EUPL-1.2: Forever Open

Even if the NeoDonkey project stopped tomorrow, the code is yours under the EUPL-1.2 license. You can fork it, modify it, sell it. It's your software. We can't take it back. We can't change the terms. We can't raise prices.
