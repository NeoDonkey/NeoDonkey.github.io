# The True Cost of SAP S/4HANA Migration: Data from Failed Projects *When the promise of a “digital core” collides with reality, the bill often comes due in ways no PowerPoint slide ever warned you about.* --- ## 1. Why Companies Chase S/4HANA (and What They Really Buy) ### 1.1 The Vendor Narrative SAP markets S/4HANA as the *next‑generation* ERP that delivers real‑time analytics, simplified data models, and a cloud‑ready foundation. The pitch is simple: **migrate once, innovate forever**. ### 1.2 The Hidden Contract What the sales decks rarely disclose is the *total cost of ownership* (TCO) that accrues long after the go‑live date. Beneath the glossy brochures lie three non‑negotiable levers that drive expense: - **License & subscription fees** – often 20‑30 % higher than ECC equivalents. - **Professional services** – implementation partners bill at $250‑$350 /hr, with typical engagements stretching 12‑24 months. - **Infrastructure & ops** – HANA‑certified hardware or cloud instances demand premium sizing (often 2‑3× the RAM/CPU of legacy systems). > **Bold insight:** *The sticker price of an S/4HANA license is merely the tip of an iceberg; the submerged mass is made up of services, hardware, and hidden operational overhead.* --- ## 2. The Vendor Lock‑In Trap: How S/4HANA Deepens Dependence ### 3.1 Licensing Mechanics - **Subscription model** (SAP S/4HANA Cloud) locks customers into annual renewal cycles with *price escalation clauses* averaging 7‑10 % YoY. - **On‑premise perpetual licenses** require *maintenance fees* of 22 % of net license value, regardless of usage. ### 3.2 Technical Dependencies | Dependency | Impact on Exit Cost | |------------|--------------------| | **HANA‑only data model** | Migration to another DB requires full re‑engineering of data structures (est. $1‑2 M per TB). | | **ABAP Stack** | Custom ABAP programs are not portable; rewriting in another language can cost 2‑3× original development. | | **SAP Fiori/UI5** | Proprietary UI framework; alternative UX platforms need complete front‑end rebuild. | | **SAP Solution Manager** | Integrated ALM tooling creates a *single‑source‑of‑truth* that is hard to replicate. | > **Bold insight:** *Leaving SAP after an S/4HANA go‑live isn’t just a contract negotiation; it’s a technical rewrite that can exceed the original migration budget.* ### 3.3 Digital Sovereignty Implications - **Data residency:** SAP’s cloud regions are limited; firms with strict data‑localization laws often must pay for private cloud or hybrid setups, inflating TCO. - **Algorithmic transparency:** SAP’s proprietary optimization engines (e.g., ATP, MRP) are black boxes, hindering audits and regulatory compliance. - **Innovation throttling:** Roadmap control resides with SAP; customers cannot prioritize features that conflict with SAP’s strategic direction. > **Bold insight:** *Vendor lock‑in erodes digital sovereignty, turning IT from a strategic enabler into a captive cost center.* --- ## 4. Strategies to Escape the S/4HANA Cost Spiral ### 5.1 Conduct a Real‑Options Analysis 1. **Model scenarios:** Stay, migrate to cloud, hybrid, or replace with open‑source. 2. **Quantify option value:** Include *exit cost* (data re‑engineering, retraining) and *flexibility premium* (ability to switch cloud providers). 3. **Decision rule:** Choose the path with the highest expected NPV *including* optionality. ### 5.2 Pilot an Open‑Source ERP in a Non‑Core Domain - **Pick a low‑risk process** (e.g., expense management, HR onboarding). - **Measure:** TCO, user adoption, integration effort. - **Scale:** If pilot meets KPIs (<10 % cost overrun, NPS >30), consider a phased rollout. ### 5.3 Negotiate SAP Contracts with Exit Clauses - **Cap annual uplift** at CPI + 2 %. - **Include data portability clause** requiring SAP to provide a canonical data dump (e.g., CSV/JSON) at no extra charge upon termination. - **Seek source‑code escrow** for critical custom ABAP to reduce rewrite risk. ### 5.4 Invest in Data Governance *Before* Migration - **Master data quality** accounts for 38 % of migration overruns. - **Deploy data‑profiling tools** (e.g., Informatica, Talend) and establish stewardship roles *six months* prior to cutover. - **Result:** Companies that pre‑cleanse data see average overruns drop from 45 % to **18 %** (Panorama 2023). ### 5.5 Build an Internal Competency Center - **Train internal ABAP/Fiori developers** rather than relying exclusively on SIs. - **Reduce hourly rate dependency** from $300/hr to $120/hr (internal fully‑loaded cost). - **Impact:** Services spend can be cut by 40‑50 % on large transformations. --- ## 6.
