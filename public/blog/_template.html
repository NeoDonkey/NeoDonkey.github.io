TITLE

DEK

Open with the specific thing that happened, or the specific thing that is true. Never open with context setting. The reader knows what an ERP is.

A section heading that states, rather than teases

One idea per paragraph. Where a claim can be checked, show the command and its real output rather than describing it. 

node demo/sarah.mjs

Then say what the output means, in one sentence, without adjectives.

Use a pull quote at most once per post, for the sentence you would want screenshotted.

What this does not solve

Every post earns its credibility here. Name the limitation plainly, link to the scorecard row it corresponds to, and do not soften it.

The code behind this post is at github.com/NeoDonkey/NeoDonkey-ERP. What works and what does not is tracked in the v1.0 scorecard.
