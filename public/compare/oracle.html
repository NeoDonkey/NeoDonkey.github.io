Why Oracle NetSuite Costs So Much

NetSuite pioneered cloud ERP and was acquired by Oracle in 2016 for $9.3 billion. That acquisition wasn't about technology — it was about recurring revenue. NetSuite's business model is designed to maximize lifetime customer value through:

  • Module upselling: Basic ERP is just the entry drug. CRM, e-commerce, PSA, and "SuiteApps" each add $$$.
  • Implementation services: Oracle partners charge $150-300/hour for setup. A typical implementation costs $50K-150K.
  • Customization dependency: SuiteScript (JavaScript-based) requires specialized consultants at $200+/hour.
  • Annual price increases: Contract typically allows 3-5% annual increases. Over 5 years, that's 16-28% higher than day one.
