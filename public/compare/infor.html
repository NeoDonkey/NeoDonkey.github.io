Frequently Asked Questions

Should I choose Infor or NeoDonkey?

If you're in a specialized industry (fashion, food & beverage, hospitality) and need deep vertical features, Infor's industry-specific modules are hard to beat. If you're a general business or professional services firm that needs core ERP (GL, AP, AR, inventory) without the complexity and cost, NeoDonkey is the better choice.

Does Infor have better reporting?

Infor includes Birst analytics and industry-specific dashboards out of the box. NeoDonkey exports to standard formats for analysis in external BI tools. Infor wins on out-of-the-box reporting; NeoDonkey wins on data portability and tool flexibility.
