The Ecosystem Lock-in Strategy

Microsoft's genius isn't the ERP, it's the integration. Your CRM talks to Outlook, which syncs with Teams, which connects to SharePoint, which feeds Power BI. Each connection is a chain. Leaving Dynamics means:

  • Rebuilding all your Excel/Outlook integrations
  • Retraining staff on new tools
  • Losing "seamless" Teams collaboration
  • Migrating Azure AD identities
  • Starting over with reporting and dashboards

This isn't accidental. Microsoft's entire business model is built on making each product essential to the others. The ERP is just the anchor tenant.

The NeoDonkey Escape Route

NeoDonkey takes the opposite approach:

  • No ecosystem: It's one tool that does ERP. It doesn't try to be your email, chat, or cloud storage.
  • Standard formats: Data is JSON in a git repo. Use any tool you want to analyze it.
  • No AI lock-in: The Copilot runs locally on your GPU. Not Microsoft's. Not OpenAI's. Yours.
  • Browser-native: No installation, no Azure tenant, no AD sync. Just a URL.
