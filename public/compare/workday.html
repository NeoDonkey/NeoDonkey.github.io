Frequently Asked Questions

Is Workday better than NeoDonkey?

For large enterprises (1,000+ employees) that need integrated HR and financials, Workday is the industry standard. For SMEs that need core ERP without the complexity and cost, NeoDonkey is the better choice. They're targeting different markets.

Does NeoDonkey have HR features?

No. NeoDonkey is a core ERP (GL, AP, AR, inventory) without HR functionality. If you need HR, integrate with dedicated HR tools or use Workday/HiBob/BambooHR alongside NeoDonkey for finance.
