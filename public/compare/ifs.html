Frequently Asked Questions

Should asset-intensive companies choose IFS or NeoDonkey?

If you're in aviation, defense, energy, or manufacturing with complex asset maintenance needs, IFS's EAM and field service modules are unmatched. NeoDonkey doesn't compete in this space, it's a core ERP for companies that don't need asset management.

Can I use NeoDonkey for service companies?

Yes, for simple service billing and project accounting. But if you need field service scheduling, mobile technician apps, and asset tracking, you'll need dedicated FSM software alongside NeoDonkey.
