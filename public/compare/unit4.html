Frequently Asked Questions

Should professional services firms choose Unit4 or NeoDonkey?

If you're a consulting firm, agency, or nonprofit that needs project accounting, time tracking, and grant management, Unit4 is designed for you. NeoDonkey is for organizations that need core financial management (GL, AP, AR) without the professional services complexity.

Can nonprofits use NeoDonkey?

Yes, for basic fund tracking and financial reporting. But if you need grant management, donor tracking, and nonprofit-specific compliance, you'll need dedicated nonprofit software alongside NeoDonkey.
