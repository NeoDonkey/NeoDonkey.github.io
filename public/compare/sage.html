Frequently Asked Questions

Is Sage better than NeoDonkey for accounting?

Sage has 40+ years of accounting heritage and deeper financial features (multi-currency revaluation, advanced consolidations, payroll). NeoDonkey covers the basics (GL, AP, AR, bank reconciliation) but doesn't match Sage's accounting depth. If you're an accountant who needs advanced features, Sage is better. If you're a business owner who needs simple, transparent bookkeeping, NeoDonkey is sufficient.

Can I migrate from Sage to NeoDonkey?

Yes. Export your chart of accounts, customer/supplier lists, and open transactions from Sage as CSV. Import them into NeoDonkey. Historical data can be preserved as signed git commits for audit purposes.
