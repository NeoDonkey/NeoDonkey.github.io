Frequently Asked Questions

Is Acumatica's pricing really unlimited users?

Acumatica doesn't charge per user, but they charge based on resource consumption (transactions, data storage, API calls). For a busy company with 50 users, this often equals €1,200-€2,400/month. NeoDonkey is truly unlimited: no per-user fees, no resource fees, no catch.

Which is better for growing companies?

Acumatica scales with your business but costs scale too. NeoDonkey scales infinitely at zero cost but requires technical expertise for customization. If you have an IT team and want maximum control, NeoDonkey. If you want managed cloud ERP with support, Acumatica.
