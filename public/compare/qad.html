Frequently Asked Questions

Should global manufacturers choose QAD or NeoDonkey?

If you're a global manufacturer with complex multi-site operations, QAD is the better choice. NeoDonkey is for companies that need core financial and inventory management without the manufacturing complexity.

Can I use NeoDonkey for multi-country operations?

NeoDonkey doesn't have built-in multi-country features (different tax regimes, transfer pricing, consolidated reporting). For global operations, you'll need dedicated global ERP software alongside NeoDonkey.
