Frequently Asked Questions

Should German mid-market companies choose ProAlpha or NeoDonkey?

ProAlpha has deep German compliance and mid-market manufacturing/wholesale expertise. NeoDonkey is for companies that need core ERP without the complexity. If you're a German manufacturer or wholesaler with 50-500 employees, ProAlpha is the safer choice. If you're smaller or want zero cost, NeoDonkey works.

Can I use NeoDonkey for wholesale?

Yes, for basic inventory and order management. But if you need advanced warehouse management, logistics, and multi-location inventory, you'll need dedicated WMS software alongside NeoDonkey.
