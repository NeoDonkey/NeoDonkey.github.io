You pay millions, wait years, and at the end you still cannot open your own data without asking.

So we made a company into a folder.

That is the whole idea. Your customers, invoices, stock and bookkeeping sit in plain files in one directory. You can open it in Finder. You can put it on a USB stick. You can email it to your accountant.

acme-gmbh/ your company
├── documents/
│ ├── article/ what you sell
│ ├── supplier/
│ ├── goods-receipt/
│ └── posting/ the bookkeeping
├── operating-model/ your rules, in plain text
│ └── processes/
└── .git/ every change, signed, forever

Nothing is hidden in a database

Every record is a readable file. If our software vanished tomorrow, your company would still be sitting there, openable.

Your rules are written down

Approval limits, discounts, who may do what. Written in plain sentences you can read, not buried in a consultant's configuration.

Nothing can be quietly changed

Every entry is signed. Alter one afterwards and the signature breaks, which is exactly what an auditor wants to hear.

Do not take our word for it. Nobody should.
